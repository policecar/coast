1

NSE ranks vectors well, but the window sits in the wrong place. Against step 1, NSE separates trained responses perfectly (|2·AUC − 1| = 1.00) on five of six datasets. But the pre-inhibition median only falls from 0.9995 to 0.988 (mnist), 0.991 (emnist) and 0.983 (kmnist) over 500k steps. The soft-WTA window switches between NSE 0.861 and 0.939, so f stays at 0.001–0.002 and the exponent at about 1.00: inhibition reduces to squaring. Only dsprites reaches 0.88, where f = 0.80. The same constant means "barely inhibit" on one dataset and "inhibit hard" on another.

2

Most alternatives are NSE on a different scale. Across all checkpoints and conditions, Spearman ρ between NSE and the coefficient of variation is −0.995 to −0.9999 on every dataset. Gini, Hoyer and Rényi-2 sort vectors the same way. Switching to one of them moves the window, not what it detects. Kurtosis, top-1 margin and robust z do measure something else, and they separate worse.

3

Scale-free measures miss what the activity level shows, and their verdict flips during training. The maximum activity separates real from pixel-shuffled digits at every checkpoint from 100k on (0.92–1.00). What changes is the margin, abruptly. Between two checkpoints the median peak response to shuffled digits falls, while the real one stays level: on mnist from 0.37 to 0.09 between 300k and 400k, on emnist from 0.39 to 0.10 between 200k and 300k, on kmnist from 0.40 to 0.16 between 100k and 200k. By 500k it is 13–18% of the real peak. NSE does not follow. Before the drop it separates the two by +0.90 to +1.00, with shuffled responses the more peaked. Afterwards it gives −0.08 (mnist), +0.02 (emnist) and −0.71 (kmnist) at 500k, where real responses are the more peaked. The dendrite attenuation already multiplies the NSE gate by (1 − σ(avg)); soft-WTA uses NSE alone.

4

Two datasets fail in ways no single-vector measure can fix. On cifar10 the group never becomes selective: real and pixel-shuffled inputs get the same responses at every checkpoint, and every measure separates them by 0.15 or less. On chairs3d the group saturates (mean activity 0.999 at 500k). NSE reads saturation as maximally flat and ranks real chairs as noisier than uniform noise.

5

The window fits images better than group vectors. Input images have NSE 0.82–0.85 (digits) against 0.974 for uniform noise, and post-inhibition group outputs, which are what a downstream dendrite reads, have medians of 0.94–0.97. Both lie around the window. Pre-inhibition vectors (≥ 0.98) do not. For binary dsprites inputs, NSE is the same for real images, shuffled images and density-matched noise (≈ 0.83); only the image-grid neighbour correlation tells them apart, and that comparison is easy because both noise types are spatially white.

6

Longer training changes the structure, not the responses. mnist and kmnist ran on to 5M steps. After the drop, the median peak response stays within 0.02 of its 500k value for real, shuffled and uniform inputs (mnist 0.68 / 0.08 / 0.21). The median NSE of real responses stays at 0.989 (mnist) and 0.985 (kmnist), still above the window. Meanwhile the dendrites keep growing. Between 500k and 5M, segments rise from 1154 to 3700 (mnist) and from 1934 to 3749 (kmnist). That approaches 3750, consistent with the cap of 50 neurons × 5 dendrites × 15 segments at max_branch_level 3. Connected synapses (permanence ≥ 0.3) rise ×2.7 and ×1.8. The only slow drift is mnist's NSE separation from shuffled input, which grows from −0.08 to −0.35. No second transition appears between the 500k and 5M checkpoints.
